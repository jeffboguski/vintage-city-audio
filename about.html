About

Gear that gets played, checked, and understood before it's sold.

Our Story

Vintage City Audio started the way most good gear collections do — one amp at a time, because it was worth saving, not because it was worth flipping. New York-based, hands-on, and built around a working knowledge of what actually makes a vintage tube circuit sound the way it does.

What comes through VCA has been played, opened up, and looked over — not just wiped down and photographed. When something needs work before it's ready to sell, that work gets done or you're told exactly what's outstanding. No surprises when it shows up.

How We Work

  • Hands-On, Not Just Listed

    Every piece gets played and checked before it's posted — not just described from a spec sheet.

  • Honest Condition Notes

    Dings, mods, and wear get called out up front. What you see in the listing is what you get in hand.

  • Full Specs, Every Time

    Serial numbers, wood, hardware, tube complement — the details that actually matter to a player or collector.

  • Local Pickup or Shipped Right

    Based in New York City. Local pickup is easy to arrange, and gear that ships gets packed like it matters.
